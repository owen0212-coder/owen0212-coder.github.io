Open the hood: methodology & limitations

FCFF grows at your annual rate through the explicit forecast. Each year’s cash flow is discounted at a constant WACC using year-end timing. Terminal value assumes perpetual growth after the last forecast year.

Enterprise value = Σ FCFFₜ / (1 + WACC)ᵗ + [FCFFₙ × (1 + g) / (WACC − g)] / (1 + WACC)ⁿ

Equity value = enterprise value + excess cash − debt and other senior claims. Value per share = equity value ÷ diluted shares. Include preferred stock, non-controlling interests, or other relevant claims in the debt/claims input as appropriate; avoid double-counting leases.

This simplified model assumes positive normalized FCFF, a stable capital structure, and a sustainable terminal growth rate. It does not separately model revenue, margins, taxes, reinvestment, future dilution, or stock-based compensation. It is unsuitable for banks or businesses with negative cash flow without a more detailed model. Negative equity outputs are left visible, not presented as literal negative stock prices.

Methodology reference: Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern: firm valuation ↗

Data, privacy & the fine print

Equity charts and headlines are embedded from TradingView with attribution. Some symbols may be delayed or unavailable under the provider’s exchange licences. We do not scrape Yahoo Finance or extract TradingView data. If an embed cannot load, use its source link. Provider data policy ↗

The Treasury chart uses a locally hosted copy of the official daily data, refreshed by the scheduled research task. The observation date and retrieval time are shown separately. A stale-data warning appears when the latest observation is over four calendar days old. Weekends and holidays can create legitimate gaps.

Analysis is authored by Codex from researched sources, not generated from the chart iframe. Briefs retain their own research cutoff and do not change when a quote changes. A daily update is scheduled for 6:30 a.m. Hong Kong time; completion depends on the scheduled task running successfully. Verify sources before relying on any claim.

Your scratchpad and DCF assumptions are stored in this browser’s local storage, not uploaded or synced. Browser clearing, private browsing, or a different device may remove or separate them. Embedded providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and are subject to their own privacy policies. Neither GitHub Pages nor external feeds are guaranteed accessible from every mainland China network.
