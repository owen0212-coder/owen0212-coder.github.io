YOUR FIRST DAY ON THE ISLAND

Build an empire.
One step at a time.

  1. Walk. Use WASD / arrows, click the ground, or tap a station sign. The Island guide walks you to any counter. On phones, use the direction pad or tap.
  2. Interact. Walk close and press E, or use the on-screen button. Tapping a sign walks you there and opens the station.
  3. Fish at the dock. Cast, hook on the bite, then hold Space or the reel button. Release before tension reaches red. Walking away abandons an unfinished attempt, not your stored catch.
  4. Sell at the fish market. Carry your catch to the green-awning stall. Collect your crew’s net proceeds there too.
  5. Visit the stockbroker. Trade fictional shares at the Exchange. Claim the one-time $100 starter grant there—no fishing unlock required.
  6. Invest in your island. Buy gear at Tackle & Co. Visit the Harbormaster for boats, land, hiring and training. New plots physically expand the island and create more crew spaces.

Crew revenue and wages are shown separately. Wages come out of crew revenue, not your wallet. Businesses and simulated stocks advance only while this tab is visible; there is no offline income, real money, or cloud save. P pauses a fishing attempt, not the whole economy.
