YOUR FIRST DAY ON THE ISLAND

Build an empire.
One step at a time.

  1. Walk. Use WASD / arrows, click the ground, or tap a station sign. The Island guide walks you to any counter. On phones, use the direction pad or tap.
  2. Interact. Walk close and press E, or use the on-screen button. Tapping a sign walks you there and opens the station.
  3. Read the float. Cast at the dock and wait through the small nibbles. When the float goes under, press Space / the button once as the timing marker enters green. Gold earns a stronger hook and a head start. Too early scares the fish; too late lets it eat the bait and escape.
  4. Win the tug of war. Hold to reel during steady swimming and recovery. Ease off when a surge is announced; release while the fish runs. Keep tension between 18% and 85%: too slack loses hook grip, too tight damages it, and 100% snaps the line. Rarer fish have tighter hook windows and stronger fights. P pauses. Release between actions; assistive clicks toggle reeling. Walking away abandons only the unfinished attempt.
  5. Sell at the fish market. Carry your catch to the green-awning stall. Collect your crew’s net proceeds there too.
  6. Visit the stockbroker. Trade fictional shares at the Exchange. Claim the one-time $100 starter grant there—no fishing unlock required.
  7. Invest in your island. Buy gear at Tackle & Co. Visit the Harbormaster for boats, land, hiring and training. New plots physically expand the island and create more crew spaces.

Crew revenue and wages are shown separately. Wages come out of crew revenue, not your wallet. Businesses and simulated stocks advance only while this tab is visible; there is no offline income, real money, or cloud save. P pauses a fishing attempt, not the whole economy.
