WELCOME TO WILLOW BAY

From one rod
to your own fleet.

  1. Cast & wait. Tap Cast, then hook the fish when it bites.
  2. Reel with care. Hold the button or Space to reel. Release to lower tension. A full red meter snaps the line.
  3. Sell & reinvest. Sell your cooler at the harbor. Upgrade your rod, bait, and storage.
  4. Build your fleet. A boat costs $450 and earns $18 each minute while you play. Collect its earnings in Fleet.

All prices are fictional game dollars. Progress stays in this browser on this device. Boats pause when the game is hidden or closed.

Tip: upgraded rods make difficult fish easier. Rare species need better gear. You can press P to pause a fishing attempt.
